Zaonus Book (Working Title)

A book I am writing in my D&D World, converted to HTML to be read online, Featuring my DMPC: Elara, a Halfling Bard who was raised by thieves and was caught before she was of age to be tried as an adult. Left in the world to fend for herself, she promised that she would be better than her parents and made an honest life for herself. Until her parents came walking through the door of the tavern she worked and performed in and turned her quiet life upside down.
