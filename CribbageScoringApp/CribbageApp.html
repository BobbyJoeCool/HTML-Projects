Cribbage Scoring App

Instructions

  • Select the 4 cards for your hand.
  • Select the card for the "cut" card.
  • Click "Score" to see the score.
  • This will fill your hand FIRST, then the cut card.
  • Crib scoring currently not supported.
  • Click RESET to reset cards and score.

Scoring Rules

  • Card values: Aces = 1, face cards = 10.
  • Fifteens: Any combination totaling 15 — 2 points.
  • Pairs: Any pair — 2 points.
    Three of a kind = 3 pairs (6 points). Four of a kind = 6 pairs (12 points).
  • Runs: Three or more consecutive cards — 1 point per card.
    Duplicate ranks multiply the run.
  • Flush: Four cards in hand of the same suit — 4 points.
    If the cut card matches the suit, score 5 points.
  • Knobs: Jack in hand matching the cut card’s suit — 1 point.
